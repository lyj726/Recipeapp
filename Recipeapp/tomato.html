Prepare ingredients

Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a medium-sized skillet over medium-high heat.
2
When oil is ready, add egg and cook until the bottom side is done, but the top is still raw, about 30 seconds.
3
Scramble the eggs while chopping into bite sized pieces, until the egg is just cooked.
4
Turn to lowest heat, transfer egg to a plate, and set aside.

1
2
F
Cook
1
Add the remaining 1/2 tablespoon of oil and the garlic and turn back to medium-high heat.
2
Stir the garlic a few times until fragrant. Add tomato and stir fry until the texture becomes soft, 1 to 2 minutes.
3
Add egg back into skillet. Sprinkle the salt and sugar evenly over everything.
4
Quickly mix everything together with a spatula until evenly seasoned.
5
Taste the dish and adjust seasoning by adding a bit more sugar or salt if needed.
